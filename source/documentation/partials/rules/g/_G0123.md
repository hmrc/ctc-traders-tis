&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;PROCEDURE’<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TRANSIT PROCEDURE’<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;THEN the Data Group &lt;CONSIGNMENT -HOUSE CONSIGNMENT -CONSIGNOR&gt; must include this<br>
